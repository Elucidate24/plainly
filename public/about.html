Why Plainly exists.

Every day people sign contracts they do not understand. We built a tool to change that.

Most people never read the full terms of a contract before signing. Not because they do not care. Because legal language is deliberately difficult to understand.

The problem we are solving

Employment contracts, rental agreements, freelance terms, software licences. These documents shape people's lives and livelihoods. They determine what you own, what you owe, what you can and cannot do, and what happens when things go wrong.

Yet most people sign them with only a vague sense of what they contain. Hiring a lawyer costs hundreds of euros per hour. Most people simply cannot afford that for every contract they encounter.

The result is that people routinely sign things that are unfair, one-sided, or simply not what they thought they were agreeing to. And by the time they find out, it is too late.

What we built

Plainly uses AI to read any legal document and explain it in plain English in seconds. Not a vague summary. A real analysis. Red flags highlighted by severity. Legal jargon explained. Missing clauses identified. A clear trust score and a direct recommendation on whether to sign.

Clear, honest, and genuinely on your side.
