Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

The short version: We never store your documents. We never read your documents. Every analysis is processed by AI and immediately forgotten. The only data we store is your email address and your monthly usage count.

Who we are

Plainly is a legal document analysis service operated by Elucidate, based in the Netherlands. You can contact us at hello@plainlyapp.com.

What data we collect

We collect only what is necessary to run the service:

  • Your email address — used to create your account and send you essential service communications.
  • Your password — stored securely and encrypted by Supabase. We never see your actual password.
  • Your monthly usage count — a single number that tracks how many analyses you have used this month. This resets at the start of each month.
  • Your subscription status — whether you are on the free or Pro plan.

What we do not collect

We do not store, log, or retain:

  • The documents you paste or upload
  • The results of your analyses
  • Any content from your legal documents
  • Your IP address
  • Your browsing behaviour within the app

How document analysis works

When you submit a document for analysis, the text is sent directly to the Anthropic API for processing. The result is returned to your screen. At no point is the document text stored on our servers or in our database. Once your browser session ends, the analysis is gone permanently.

Anthropic, the company that powers our AI analysis, has their own privacy policy available at anthropic.com. They process the document text to generate the analysis but do not retain it for training purposes under their current API terms.

Payments

Payments are processed by Lemon Squeezy, who act as the merchant of record. We do not store your payment card details. Lemon Squeezy's privacy policy governs how your payment data is handled. You can find it at lemonsqueezy.com.

Your rights under GDPR

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Correct inaccurate data
  • Delete your account and all associated data at any time from the Settings screen
  • Object to processing of your data
  • Lodge a complaint with the Dutch Data Protection Authority (Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens)

To exercise any of these rights, email us at hello@plainlyapp.com.

Data storage

Your account data is stored securely by Supabase on servers within the European Union. We retain your account data for as long as your account exists. When you delete your account, all associated data is permanently removed within 30 days.

Cookies

We use only essential cookies required for authentication. We do not use tracking cookies, advertising cookies, or analytics cookies.

Changes to this policy

If we make significant changes to this policy we will notify you by email. Continued use of Plainly after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Email us at hello@plainlyapp.com.
